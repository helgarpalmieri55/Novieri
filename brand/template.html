01 — the idea
novieri

Warmth and rigor, at the same time.

Novieri is family-run — the name fuses the two founders’ surnames — and holds enterprise standards. That pairing is the brand. Not a friendly company that happens to be technical, and not a technical company that has learned to smile: both at once, in every artifact.

Personality

Premium. Jewel. Distinctive. Lean into the logo’s gem language — jewel-tone commitment, gold used with intent, drama in deliberate moments. The reference for precision is Linear: engineered layouts, tight grids, mono details that speak to a technical buyer.

Voice

A senior engineer who explains clearly. Confident, plain, specific — never a sales deck. Sentence case everywhere. Spanish uses tú: warm, direct, professional. Colombian Spanish, never a calque of the English.

The five principles

  • The logo is the law. Every color derives from the mark’s teal → plum → gold. Ornament is rationed to two motifs.
  • Depth from borders, not shadows. 1px hairlines and background steps. No drop shadows, no glow, no blur.
  • One accent, deliberate doses. Plum carries interaction. Gold appears only where the eye should land.
  • Typography does the selling. If a section feels flat, fix it with type and spacing — never with more color.
  • Every claim is specific. Real timezone math, real certifications, real package names. No vague promises.

02 — the mark
novieri

A faceted bloom with a star at its center.

clear space = 45% of the mark’s width, on all four sides

Construction

The gradient runs teal → plum → gold along the diagonal, matching the light source implied by the facets. It is the one place a gradient may appear as a fill. Never recolor the mark, never flatten it to a single tone, and never redraw the facets.

Minimum size

24px on screen, 10mm in print. Below that the facets close up and the star at the center stops reading.

Lock-up

Mark and wordmark sit on a shared baseline with a gap of 10px at a 48px mark. The wordmark is Clash Display 600, lowercase, letter-spacing −0.02em, in --ink. It is never set in another face.

03 — color
novieri

White and black carry the page. Jewels do the work on top.

Every ratio below is measured, not estimated. WCAG AA needs 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text; AAA needs 7:1.

ink — text on white
accents on white

Proportion

white and ink ~86%  ·  plum-wash ~8%  ·  plum ~4%  ·  gold ~2%

Gold is the rarest thing on the page and should stay that way — it marks where the eye is meant to land, and a page with gold in four places has told the reader nothing. Plum is reserved for interaction; if something is plum, it should be clickable.

03 — color, continued
novieri

The dark stage.

Black sections are moments, not the default: the hero, the assistant panel, the call-to-action band, the footer. Each accent swaps to its bright twin so it does not sink into the ground.

The gradient rule

A gradient is permitted in exactly three places: the logo artwork, the gem’s core and facets, and the 2px seam that joins a dark section to a light one. Never on text. Never as a background fill. Never on a button.

the seam — 2px, teal → plum → gold

Where the stage appears

  • The home hero, and nowhere else above the fold.
  • The assistant panel — the product showing itself.
  • The call-to-action band before the footer.
  • The footer.

What the seam is

A structural joint, not decoration. It marks the boundary where the page changes state from light to dark, and it earns its gradient by being 2px tall. A seam between two sections of the same color is residue — remove it.

05 — signature elements
novieri

Two marks, rationed.

··

The two dots

Opens every eyebrow, marks every list item, numbers every index as ··01. Gold on white, gold-bright on black. It is the single most recognizable thing the brand owns after the mark itself.

✦

The four-point star

The gem’s core. Used as a separator in marquees and as ornament on dark bands. Never as a bullet — that is the dots’ job — and never more than once in a viewport.

The per-pillar accent

Each service pillar owns a color, carried by the ·· marks on that pillar’s pages and nothing else. Interaction stays plum everywhere, on every pillar — a second interaction color would compete with the first.

06 — layout & motion
novieri

Depth from borders.

Structure

  • Max width 1240px, centerd.
  • Section padding clamp(3.5rem, 9vh, 6rem).
  • Reading measure 640px for long-form, at 19px.
  • Cards: 1px hairline, 16px radius, no shadow.
  • Touch targets never below 44px.

Motion

  • Reveal on scroll: 16px rise, 600ms, 60–90ms stagger.
  • Hover: cards lift 4–6px, buttons 2px, arrows slide.
  • The gem breathes continuously; nothing else loops.
  • All animation is gated behind html.js — content is visible without JavaScript.
  • prefers-reduced-motion collapses every animation to zero and sets revealed content to full opacity. Never strand content at opacity 0.

Anti-references — what this brand is not

visualGradient text. Glassmorphism. Glow. Drop-shadow cards. Hero-metric templates. Carousels. Video heroes.
imageryStock photos of handshakes, headsets, or people pointing at screens. Logo-soup trust bars.
copyBuzzwords — synergy, empower, revolutionary. Exclamation marks. Title Case headlines. Vague promises.

Controls

Book a call Secondary tag Text link →

Radius 10px on buttons, 16px on cards, 6px on tags. Primary is plum with white text at 10.52:1. Secondary is a hairline, never a second fill color.

07 — voice
novieri

Say the specific thing.

The test for any sentence: could a competitor publish it unchanged? If yes, it is not finished.

do — from the live site “Most firms make you sit through a sales call before you hear a price. Here is ours up front.”

“Two things happen before you answer, and we would rather write them down than let you discover them.”

“AI answers can be wrong. Don’t share passwords or payment details here.”
don’t “We leverage cutting-edge AI to empower your digital transformation journey!”

“Industry-leading solutions tailored to your unique needs.”

“Contact us today to learn more!”

English

  • US spelling and conventions. 1,000+, not 1000+.
  • Sentence case in headlines, navigation and buttons.
  • Em dashes for asides, not semicolons.

Español

  • Colombian Spanish, tú, never usted and never a calque.
  • Sentence case, and accents always: Consultoría, never Consultoria.
  • Numbers in local format: +1.000, $95.000.

One verb per destination

Every control that promises a meeting says Book a call / Agendar llamada and goes to the calendar. Nothing else uses a booking verb. A label that promises a booking and lands somewhere else is a bug, and the build fails on it.

Naming a page, a product, a menu item

Navigation is where these conventions slip first, because menu items are typed by hand in a CMS rather than written in a file with the rest of the copy.
