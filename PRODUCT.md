# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences, one brand:

1. **US SMB / startup decision-makers** (founders, ops leads, CTOs of companies without an internal IT department), evaluating nearshore providers from a browser tab, in English (`/en`). They compare against US agencies and offshore body shops; the job to be done is "find a firm I can trust with my infrastructure, security, and AI roadmap without US-agency prices."
2. **Barranquilla / Colombian SMB owners and managers**, in Spanish (`/es`), who need enterprise-level technology (security, monitoring, AI, real processes) without building an internal IT department. Warm, direct, "tú" voice; they buy trust in the founders, not a brand wall.

Both arrive from referrals, LinkedIn, or search; the site's job is to establish senior-engineer credibility fast and convert to a booked call or WhatsApp conversation.

## Product Purpose

Bilingual (ES default / EN) marketing site for Novieri, an AI-first IT solutions company in Barranquilla, Colombia, serving Colombian companies locally and US companies remotely (nearshore). Four service pillars in strict priority order: AI & Automation → Managed IT → Cybersecurity & Compliance → Custom Software. Success = booked calls (Cal.com), WhatsApp conversations (ES), and contact form submissions.

## Brand Personality

Confident, plain, specific. A senior engineer who explains clearly — never a sales deck. No buzzword salad, no exclamation marks, sentence case everywhere. Spanish uses "tú", warm but professional. The company is family-run (the name fuses the founders' two surnames) with enterprise standards — that pairing of warmth and rigor IS the brand.

Visual identity comes from the final logo: a faceted, gem-like geometric bloom with a four-point star at its center, in a teal → plum → gold gradient, with a dark-plum lowercase wordmark. Jewel-tone premium, not startup-blue tech.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages: hero-metric templates, gradient text, glassmorphism, glow, drop-shadow cards.
- Stock photos of handshakes, headsets, people pointing at screens.
- "Ultramarine tech-startup blue" — the brief's original placeholder accent; superseded by the final logo palette.
- Logo soup trust bars, carousels, video heroes.
- Buzzword copy ("synergy", "empower", exclamation marks).
- MSP/agency sites that look like every other MSP/agency site.

## Design Principles

1. **The logo is the law.** Every color on the site derives from the final logo's teal–plum–gold; ornament is rationed to the two-dot / four-point-star motifs.
2. **Depth from borders, not shadows.** 1px hairlines and paper→surface background steps; light mode only for v1.
3. **One accent, deliberate doses.** Plum carries interaction (links, CTAs); gold appears only where the eye should land (a key stat, the star, the i-dots).
4. **Typography does the selling.** If a section feels flat, fix it with type and spacing, never with more color.
5. **Every claim is specific.** Real time-zone math, real certifications-in-progress, real package names — no vague promises.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: 4.5:1 body contrast (verify plum/gold on paper; use deep variants for small text), visible focus states (accent outline), full keyboard navigation, one h1 per page, `prefers-reduced-motion` disables all animation, both locales complete with hreflang alternates.
