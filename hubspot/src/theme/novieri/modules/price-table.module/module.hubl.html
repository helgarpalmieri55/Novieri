{# One pillar's price anchors, in both currencies at once. The currency follows the visitor's location, not the page's language — the owner's rule, and the right one: a Spanish-speaking buyer in Miami budgets in dollars, an English-speaking one in Bogotá in pesos. module.hubl.js reads the timezone (America/Bogota → COP, anywhere else → USD), remembers a manual choice, and shows one price per row. A row with no price in the active currency disappears whole: the WhatsApp bot is priced for Colombia, the fractional CTO for the US. Every figure is a range or a floor, never a quote — the copy that feeds this says so, because the exact number depends on the business and arrives with the proposal. Five of these stack on the page, hence padding tighter than section-pad. #}
